    How it works

    1. Pick a shop

      Each shop is a complete store with its own sections, products, bag and checkout.

    2. Walk around

      Tap or double-tap the floor to walk there and drag to look around; each shop shows you how when you arrive. Tap a product for its details, or use the directory and search.

    3. Try it on

      See frames, clothes and jewellery on yourself with your camera. You can use a photo instead.

    Your camera stays on your device. Try-on video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded or recorded.

    A virtual marketplace by Plexus Digitals

    Sample shops for demonstration: products, prices and stock are placeholders, and checkout takes no payment.

    The camera try-on needs a secure (https) connection on phones and laptops.
